ABOUT CHEMATRIX SOLUTIONS

Engineering Intelligence. Digital Transformation.

Chematrix Solutions is your trusted engineering and technology partner for advanced Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) consultancy, process engineering, and manufacturing digitalisation. We provide innovative, practical, and cost-effective solutions that help manufacturing and process industries solve complex engineering challenges, optimise performance, improve productivity, and accelerate digital transformation.

Advanced CFD & Engineering Solutions

At Chematrix Solutions, we understand that accurate engineering analysis and intelligent digital solutions play a critical role in modern product development and manufacturing. Our expertise in CFD simulation and engineering analysis enables businesses to understand complex fluid-flow and thermal behaviour, optimise equipment and processes, reduce development time, and make better engineering decisions before implementing physical changes.

Our CFD consultancy and simulation services cover a wide range of industrial applications, including fluid flow, heat transfer, thermal analysis, multiphase flow, mixing, combustion, ventilation, equipment optimisation, and process simulation.

We support multidisciplinary industries including chemical, petrochemical, manufacturing, automotive, aerospace, energy, process industries, and industrial equipment across India and the Middle East.

Manufacturing Digitalisation

Beyond engineering simulation, Chematrix Solutions helps organisations transform traditional manufacturing workflows through process digitalisation and digital transformation.

Our digitalisation services include custom engineering software development, process automation, data analytics, interactive dashboards, workflow digitisation, AI/ML solutions, digital engineering tools, and data-driven decision support systems.

We help convert manual and repetitive processes into smarter, connected, automated, and scalable digital workflows that improve operational visibility, productivity, quality, and decision-making.
