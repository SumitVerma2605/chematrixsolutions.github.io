Who We Are

A team that speaks both fluid dynamics and software

Chematrix Solutions was founded on a simple observation: process industries generate enormous engineering knowledge, but too much of it stays locked in spreadsheets, reports and individual expertise. We exist to change that — by simulating the physics rigorously, and then digitalizing what we learn so it keeps working long after the project ends.

Every engagement starts with the same question an experienced process engineer would ask: what is actually happening inside the equipment? From there, we bring in CFD, process fundamentals, and — where it adds real value — software and data science, to turn that understanding into something your team can use daily.

Engineering Philosophy

First principles first — digital second

We don't sell software for its own sake, and we don't hand over a CFD report and walk away. Our philosophy is that good digital tools are only as good as the physics and process understanding underneath them.

Simulate rigorously

Numerical simulation grounded in real boundary conditions and validated engineering judgment — not default solver settings.

Translate practically

Findings are communicated in terms operators, engineers and plant managers can act on — not just contour plots.

Digitalize deliberately

Software and dashboards are built only where they genuinely reduce manual effort or improve decision speed.
